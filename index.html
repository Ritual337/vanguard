THE SHORT VERSION No company. No roster. A few friends, one alliance, and a standing invitation to the next push.

Vanguard is a small circle of friends who met in Warpath, the modern-war strategy game by Lilith Games. We picked a camp, joined one alliance, and kept showing up. There is no company behind this page and no membership form. If you have been in the chat, you are already part of it.

Warpath is a real-time strategy game about building a base, training an army, and holding ground with other people. It has three camps — Camp Liberty, Vanguard Division and Martyrs' Watch — each with its own tanks, artillery and aircraft. You pick one, and then you find out the game is really about the people you play it with.

Our alliance is small and unglamorous. Someone scouts, someone hoards resources, someone forgets a timer and loses a garrison at three in the morning. We do the events when we can, cover each other's bases when we cannot, and nobody keeps score once the game is closed.

That is the whole organisation chart: a camp, an alliance, a chat, and friends who come back the next night. The war is fictional. The group is not.

The game is the excuse. The group is the point.
